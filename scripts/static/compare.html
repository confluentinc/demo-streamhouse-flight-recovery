Flink Centralized stream processing

Fully managed SQL that the whole business shares. Connection risk is computed once, and Lightning Tables serves it to any app or agent. Results arrive at each Flink checkpoint, about once a minute.

Kafka Streams Stream processing inside your app

A library in your own service: you own the full lifecycle. The app queries its own local state, so a change shows up in under a second. You run it, and Confluent helps: the Kafka Streams UI shows its topology, health, and where to look when something's off.
